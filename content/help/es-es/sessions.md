## Where to manage sessions

Open **Account → Security → Sessions** to see devices and browsers with active sign-ins.

## Ending sessions

You can revoke individual sessions or sign out everywhere except your current browser.

## Fresh session for sensitive actions

Some destructive actions, such as **account deletion**, require a recently created session for extra protection.
