## Moving items to trash

Deleting a file or folder moves it to **Trash** first. You can restore items while they remain in the retention window.

## Seven-day retention

Trashed items are purged automatically after **seven days**, which frees quota. Organization owners may receive additional purge actions in future releases.

## Restore

Open **Account → Storage → Trash** and choose **Restore** on any row still inside the window.
