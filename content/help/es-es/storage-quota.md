## Org-scoped storage

Files and folders belong to the **active organization**. Usage meters include live blobs and items in trash until they are purged.

## Upload limits

When quota is exceeded, new uploads are blocked. Reads and downloads for existing files continue for members with access.

## Owners and grants

Organization owners manage all files. Members see folders and files shared through **grants** issued by the owner.
