## Active organization

Many Account features use your **active organization**, including storage, AI usage, and billing. Switch orgs from the sidebar org switcher.

## Creating an org

Free plans allow **one organization per user**. Owners complete setup steps before members can collaborate.

## Invitations

Owners send email invites from **Organization → Invitations**. Recipients accept with the same sign-in methods allowed by your workspace.
