## Roles

- **Owner** — full org control, billing, storage grants, invitations.
- **Manager** — member management where enabled; no billing ownership.
- **Member** — uses resources granted by the owner.

## Billing visibility

Billing management is **hidden from non-owners**. Members may still see subscription entitlements on read-only screens.

## Cross-organization access

If you are not a member of an organization, APIs return **403** — switch to an org where you belong.
