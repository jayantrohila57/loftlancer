## Before you upgrade

Confirm you are the **organization owner** and have an active organization selected.

## What improves on Paid

Paid plans unlock higher storage quota, AI credits, and billing self-service. Entitlements sync from billing after checkout completes.

## Need help?

If checkout fails, return to **Billing → Overview** and try the portal again, or contact support from **Help & Support → Support Tickets**.
