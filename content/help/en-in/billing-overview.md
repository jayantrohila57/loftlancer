## Plans

Your organization is on a **Free** or **Paid** plan. Owners manage upgrades and payment methods in **Account → Billing**.

## Invoices and portal

Paid organizations can open the billing portal from **Payment methods** or **Invoices** to update cards and download receipts.

## Who can manage billing

Only **organization owners** see billing management tabs. Other members may still see read-only subscription entitlements where product rules allow.
