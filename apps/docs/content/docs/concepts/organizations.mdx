---
title: Organizations
description: Multi-tenant workspaces, membership, and org-scoped API context.
---

An **organization** is the top-level tenant boundary. Members belong to exactly one org role at a time (owner, manager, or member). Projects, teams, invitations, and org settings are scoped to an organization.

## In the product

- The studio app uses an **active organization** cookie for browser sessions.
- tRPC procedures call `requireActiveOrganization` and evaluate actions with org context.
- MCP tools accept `organizationId` or `organizationSlug` on each call because bearer tokens do not carry the active-org cookie (`organizationRefSchema` in `@loftlancer/orpc/mcp`).

## Related actions

Examples from `@loftlancer/actions`: `organization.list`, `organization.view`, `organization.create`, `organization.update`, `organization.delete`, `organization.settings.view`, `organization.transfer_ownership`.
